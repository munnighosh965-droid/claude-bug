## 🎮 How to Use:

1. **Click "TAP TO ENTER"** on the first screen
2. **Video will play** with audio fade-in
3. **Explore the interface:**
   - Top-right: Navigation menu
   - Bottom-left: Brand tagline
   - Bottom-right: "ENTER THE DROP" button
   - Bottom-right corner: Video controls

4. **Try these interactions:**
   - Click video once → Ripple effect
   - Double-click video → Replay
   - Click `+` hotspots → View products (appear at 3s, 8s, 15s)
   - Scroll down → Video scales and transitions to products
   - Use control buttons → Play/pause, sound, replay, fullscreen

5. **Shop the collection:**
   - Browse products below the video
   - Click products for quick view
   - Add to bag
   - Save to wishlist

## 🆘 Need Help?

### Video not showing?
- Check the video file is in the same folder as index.html
- Try refreshing the page (Ctrl+R or Cmd+R)

### No sound?
- Click "TAP TO ENTER" first
- Check the sound button (bottom-right controls)
- Check your browser isn't muting the tab

### Performance issues?
- Close other browser tabs
- Try a different browser
- Video will auto-pause when tab is inactive
